About Pixie Painting

Pixie Painting is a small, family-style painting and decorating business based in Dorset and Hampshire, dedicated to transforming homes and workspaces with care, precision, and a touch of magic.

Request a Quote See Our Work

Our Story

Pixie Painting was created by Aysel, a highly skilled and experienced painter and decorator with a keen eye for detail and a genuine love for what she does. After years working in the industry, Aysel decided to establish her own business so she could offer clients the personal attention and quality craftsmanship that bigger companies often overlook.

The name “Pixie” reflects our philosophy: we bring a little sparkle and transformation to every project. Like a pixie waving a wand, we help tired or dated spaces feel brand new — refreshed, vibrant, and full of life.

What We Care About

  • Prep first: sanding, filling, masking and protecting surfaces.
  • Clean work: tidy each day, minimal disruption, respect your space.
  • Quality finish: good materials + careful technique = long lasting results.
  • Clear communication: honest quotes, updates, and realistic timelines.
